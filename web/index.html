Load your Cybiko Xtreme dumps

The emulator needs two files from a Cybiko Xtreme. This site does not host them: pick your own copies. They stay in this browser and are never uploaded.

  1. Boot ROM, cyrom150.bin (32 KB), and flash with CyOS 1.5.08, cyos_v1508.bin (512 KB). Both are in MAME's Cybiko ROM set (cybikov1.zip, listed for example on Planet Emulation), and kept in cybiko-roms. Pick the zip and the page takes the two files out of it, or pick them one by one.
  2. Optional: a CyOS update (*.boot, such as the Russian CyOS 1.5.08), installed on start.

PC keys: letters, Enter, Esc, Space, arrows, Tab, Backspace = Del, Shift, Ctrl = Fn, F1-F7, F8 or Home = Select, F9 = Help, F10 or Insert = Ins, F11 = Menu, F12 = On/Off, [ ] = ( ), / = !. Drop .app or other files on the page (or use Add files) to put them on the Cybiko; it restarts to take them.

Where to get apps, games and the MP3 player

cybiko-roms keeps a copy of all of it: the dumps, both CDs, the MP3 cartridge software and clips, and the Russian CyOS.

  • Games and apps (.app, with their .dat or other files if they have any): the cybiko.net download mirror, Kerry Brown's archive and Jake Kussmaul's collection on the Internet Archive. Drop them on the page or use Add files to the Cybiko. They appear at the end of the Main desktop after the restart.
  • Installers (.cap, .csu) from the Xtreme Installation CD, such as CyRecord, CyTalk or E-mail: add them the same way. The page unpacks them, as EZ Loader did.
  • The MP3 cartridge needs its software on the Cybiko: MP3.cap from the MP3 player cartridge's own CD (here), added with Add files to the Cybiko. The MP3.cap on the Xtreme Installation CD is an older version that never finds the card. Then put MP3s and MXD clips on the card in Settings → MP3 cartridge. "MP3 Player" appears on the desktop; in it, Menu opens the song list.
  • Everything on the Cybiko is in Settings → Files on the Cybiko, and Download backup saves all of it in one file.

Settings

Gives the CyWIG gate its internet and links this Cybiko to others in the same room. Empty: no internet, radio only between tabs of this browser.

Cybikos in the same room hear each other (Chat, games). Tabs of this browser share it without the relay.

Card:

MP3s and MXD clips (.app files made by the converters), as you would put them on the real card. Kept in this browser; the Cybiko sees them after a restart. The cartridge's software (MP3.cap) goes on the Cybiko, not the card: the page moves it there if you add it here.
