Start your Cybiko

The emulator runs the Cybiko's own firmware: its boot ROM and CyOS 1.5.08. One click fetches both from cybiko-roms on GitHub, and this browser keeps them.

The emulator runs the first Cybiko's own firmware: its boot ROM and CyOS 1.2.46, the dumps of MAME's cybikov1 set, as ReBiko ships them. One click fetches both from cybiko-roms on GitHub, and this browser keeps them.

Use my own files instead

Pick the boot ROM, cyrom150.bin (32 KB), and the flash, cyos_v1508.bin (512 KB), or MAME's Cybiko ROM set (cybikov1.zip), from which the page takes those two. A CyOS update (*.boot) is optional and installed on start. Nothing is uploaded.

Pick the boot ROM, cyrom112.bin (32 KB), and the flash, flash_v1246.bin (528 KB), or a zip with them: MAME's cybikov1.zip, or a ReBiko release, which has them as roms/boot.bin and roms/flash.bin. A saved flash_saved.bin (1 MB) works as the flash too. Nothing is uploaded.

PC keys: letters, Enter, Esc, Space, arrows, Tab, Backspace = Del, Shift, Ctrl = Fn, F1-F7, F8 or Home = Select, F9 = Help, F10 or Insert = Ins, F11 = Menu, F12 = On/Off, [ ] = ( ), / = !. Drop .app or other files on the page (or use Add files) to put them on the Cybiko; it restarts to take them.

Get apps, games and the MP3 player

Tick what you want and press Install: the files come from cybiko-roms, copies of the Cybiko CDs. The Cybiko restarts to take them, and new apps appear at the end of the Main desktop.

More games and apps: the cybiko.net mirror, Kerry Brown's archive and Jake Kussmaul's collection. Download them, then drop them on the page or use Add files to the Cybiko; .cap and .csu installers are unpacked. Everything on the Cybiko is in Settings → Files on the Cybiko.

Settings

Card:

    MP3s and MXD clips (.app files made by the converters), as you would put them on the real card. Kept in this browser; the Cybiko sees them after a restart. What the Cybiko does on the card (its File Manager's Delete, files it writes) is kept too. The cartridge's software (MP3.cap) goes on the Cybiko, not the card: the page moves it there if you add it here.

    The Classic's backup is its flash (flash_saved.bin, as the board saves it): CyOS and its files together.
